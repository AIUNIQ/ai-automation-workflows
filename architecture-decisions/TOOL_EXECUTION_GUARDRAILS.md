# Tool Execution Guardrails

## Problem

AI systems may incorrectly imply that actions have been completed when they have not.

## Decision

AI agents must never simulate successful business actions.

## Required Process

Request

↓

Validation

↓

Tool Execution

↓

Success Response

↓

Customer Confirmation

## Benefits

- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Customer trust
- Operational integrity

## Lesson

Execution results must come from systems, not assumptions.
