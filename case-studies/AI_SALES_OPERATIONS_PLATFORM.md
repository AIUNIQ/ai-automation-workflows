# AI Sales Operations Platform

## Business Challenge

Create an AI-powered system capable of handling customer conversations, workflow execution, CRM synchronization, membership management, notifications, and operational monitoring.

## Architecture Components

### AI Layer

- Conversational AI
- Intent Detection
- Decision Routing
- Response Generation

### Communication Layer

- Voice Interactions
- SMS Messaging
- Automated Follow-Ups

### Automation Layer

- Workflow Orchestration
- Event Processing
- Business Rule Validation

### CRM Layer

- Contact Management
- Segmentation
- Lifecycle Tracking

### Operations Layer

- Notifications
- Monitoring
- Escalation Handling

## Engineering Goals

- Reduce manual work
- Improve consistency
- Maintain compliance
- Increase operational visibility
- Scale customer interactions

## Lessons

- Tool execution must be validated.
- Operational truth comes from successful system responses.
- AI should not assume business actions completed successfully.
