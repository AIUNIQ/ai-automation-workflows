# AI Sales & Customer Operations Platform

## Overview

Designed and documented an AI-powered customer operations platform integrating conversational AI, workflow automation, CRM synchronization, membership management, and operational monitoring.

## Architecture Components

### Conversational Layer

- ElevenLabs Conversational AI
- Human-style conversational design
- Consent-gated transaction handling

### Communication Layer

- Twilio Voice
- Twilio SMS
- Automated outreach workflows

### Middleware Layer

- Zapier
- Webhook orchestration
- Conditional workflow routing
- Validation gates

### CRM Layer

- Ontraport
- Contact management
- Segmentation
- Tag automation

### Commerce Layer

- SamCart
- Subscription management
- Refund processing
- Membership upgrades
- Billing workflows

### Monitoring Layer

- Slack alerts
- Operational notifications
- Audit visibility

## Engineering Focus

- AI Agents
- Workflow Automation
- CRM Integration
- Webhook Architecture
- Consent Management
- Transaction Integrity
- Operational Reliability

## Key Design Principle

The AI never assumes success.

Operational truth comes only from successful tool execution and transactional system responses.
