# Consent-Driven Automation

## Problem

Business actions involving customer accounts require explicit authorization before execution.

## Design

Customer Request

↓

Consent Request

↓

Verification

↓

Authorization

↓

Workflow Execution

↓

Confirmation

## Key Principle

No action is executed until explicit authorization is captured.

## Benefits

- Compliance
- Auditability
- Customer protection
- Reduced disputes
- Transaction integrity

## Engineering Focus

- State management
- Validation logic
- Workflow control
- Audit logging
