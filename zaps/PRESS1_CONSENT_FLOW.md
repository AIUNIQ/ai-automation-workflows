# Press 1 Consent Workflow

## Objective

Capture explicit customer authorization before executing a business action.

## Flow

AI Conversation

↓

Offer Presented

↓

Consent Requested

↓

Customer Presses 1

↓

Consent Validated

↓

Audit Log Created

↓

Business Action Executed

↓

Confirmation Sent

## Design Principles

- Explicit authorization required
- Compliance-first workflow
- Auditability
- Transaction integrity

## Benefits

- Clear customer authorization
- Reduced disputes
- Reliable compliance records
- Controlled workflow execution
