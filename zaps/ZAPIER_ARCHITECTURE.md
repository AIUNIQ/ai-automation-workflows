# Zapier Automation Architecture

## Purpose

Coordinate automation workflows between business systems, AI services, communication platforms, and operational tools.

## Core Components

### Trigger Systems

- SamCart
- Forms
- CRM Events
- Webhooks

### Processing Layer

- Zapier
- Business Rules
- Conditional Routing

### Operational Systems

- Ontraport
- Slack
- Twilio
- Email Platforms

### AI Systems

- OpenAI
- AI Agents
- Classification Workflows
