# Membership Cancellation Workflow

## Objective

Process membership cancellation requests through a controlled workflow.

## Flow

Customer Requests Cancellation

↓

Identity Verification

↓

Subscription Lookup

↓

Cancellation Validation

↓

Membership Updated

↓

CRM Updated

↓

Slack Notification

↓

Confirmation Sent

## Operational Rules

- Verify customer identity
- Validate active membership
- Execute cancellation through system of record
- Confirm only after successful completion

## Benefits

- Consistent cancellation process
- Reduced manual work
- Improved auditability
