# Post Purchase Alert Flow

## Objective

Notify internal systems and operations teams immediately after a successful purchase.

## Workflow

SamCart Purchase

↓

Zapier Trigger

↓

Ontraport Contact Update

↓

Slack Notification

↓

Twilio SMS

↓

Customer Follow-Up Workflow

## Benefits

- Immediate operational visibility
- Automated customer engagement
- Reduced manual processing
- Consistent workflow execution
